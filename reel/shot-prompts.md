# Patte mein basa Suraj — shot prompts

Copy-paste prompts for turning this animatic into finished frames with an image or video model
(Veo, Kling, Runway, Pika, Midjourney, Firefly — anything). Every prompt is written for **vertical
9:16, 1080 × 1920**.

Two rules that matter more than the prompt text:

1. **Generate shot 01 first, then feed it back as a style reference** for every other shot. Madhubani
   consistency comes from the reference image, not from adjectives.
2. **Generate stills, then animate with image-to-video.** Text-to-video invents new faces every take;
   image-to-video keeps Maa, Dadi and Gudiya the same people.

---

## Global style block (prepend to every prompt)

> Traditional Madhubani / Mithila folk painting, flat poster colours, thick black ink outlines, double
> contour lines, patterned fills (dots, cross-hatch, fish-scale), limited palette of ivory, saffron,
> vermilion, leaf green and indigo. Hand-painted on textured paper. Frontal, stylised figures with big
> almond eyes and gold jewellery. No gradients, no photorealism, no 3D render, no text, no watermark.
> Vertical 9:16 composition, 1080×1920, generous negative space for a caption.

**Negative prompt:** photorealistic, 3d render, cgi, anime, western cartoon, glossy, lens flare, bokeh,
blurry, extra fingers, distorted face, text, letters, watermark, logo, frame border, split screen,
collage, grid.

## The 12 shots

### 01 · 0:00 – 0:04 — *Kelwa ke paat*
> Macro close-up, the entire frame filled by one giant banana leaf painted in leaf-green with ivory
> veins and black outlines. Its veins flow and curve like river waves; a single golden dew-drop rests on
> the leaf, and inside the drop a tiny sun burns vermilion and gold. Two smaller leaves enter at the
> corners, a pink lotus and two small fish decorate the green. Flat Madhubani painting, glowing drop.
>
> **Motion (i2v):** slow push-in, veins shimmer like water, the drop rolls 2 cm and settles, the tiny sun
> inside pulses once.

### 02 · 0:04 – 0:09 — *Boond mein suraj*
> Medium close-up of a young girl (Gudiya, 8) painted in Madhubani style, vermilion saree, gold blouse,
> two thin black plaits with gold ties, large almond eyes, bindi. She holds a glowing golden drop in
> cupped hands; the drop contains a tiny sun. From the left edge of frame, her mother's forearm enters,
> offering three marigolds. A pale sun disc and a dashed leaf-vein path to the horizon behind her.
> Ivory background.
>
> **Motion:** the reflection of the sun moves inside her eye, her lashes lower once, the marigolds sway.

### 03 · 0:09 – 0:17 — *Soop sajta hai*
> Courtyard of a mud house, terracotta wall with a white painted band of fish and lotus motifs, an arched
> doorway with an oil lamp, sugarcane stalks on the right, a marigold garland strung across the top.
> Two women — Maa in red, Dadi in green with grey hair — stand on either side of a large golden bamboo
> soop (offering tray) on a wooden stool. Inside: thekua, banana, coconut, fruit, marigolds, small lotus
> and fish motifs. A little girl in pink places a flower. Rangoli on the floor.
>
> **Motion:** one new item lands in the tray on each musical beat, tray contents settle, garland sways.

### 04 · 0:17 – 0:25 — *Ghat ki raah*
> Side view, Madhubani village at dusk: mud houses with thatched roofs and painted walls, round green
> trees, a saffron and vermilion banded sky, a half-set sun with triangular rays. Three figures walk
> right in profile-ish flat style: Dadi in green carries a gold daura on her head, Maa in red holds
> Gudiya's hand, Gudiya in pink swings a small flower. An ivory dashery path runs to the river at the
> far right; the leaf-vein road turns into water.
>
> **Motion:** side-scrolling camera, trees and houses loop past, walking cycle bob, dupattas flutter.

### 05 · 0:25 – 0:33 — *Ghat ka reveal*
> Wide shot of a Chhath ghat at sunset. Layered flat sky bands from cream to saffron to vermilion, a
> huge half-sunken sun with a gentle face and triangular rays, teal river with shimmering gold
> reflection dashes, wide stone steps descending on the right, small oil lamps with warm halos floating
> near the bank. Tiny figures on the steps: a woman holds a soop, a girl beside her, an elder with folded
> hands. Lotus and fish at the bottom edge.
>
> **Motion:** very slow push-in toward the soop (max 8%), water ripples travel, lamp flames flicker.

### 06 · 0:33 – 0:45 — *Sandhya arghya*
> Devotional wide shot: Maa stands waist-deep in the teal river, arms lifted, holding a golden soop of
> offerings exactly in front of the setting sun so the tray is silhouetted against the sun's disc. Dadi
> and Gudiya stand on the ochre bank at right, hands folded in namaste. Concentric ripples around Maa's
> waist; lotus and fish patterns open in the water. Sky in deep saffron and vermilion.
>
> **Motion:** hold the shot — no cuts. The tray lifts 3 cm, ripples expand, Gudiya's folded hands tremble
> slightly, the sun sinks a hair.

### 07 · 0:45 – 0:48 — *Suraj doobta hai*
> The sun's last sliver sliding behind the horizon line, sky in violet, rose, vermilion and saffron bands,
> a narrow gold reflection path on dark teal water, two birds as small ink strokes. Lotus in the
> foreground corner.
>
> **Motion:** the sun descends and flattens, the reflection thins and breaks up.

### 08 · 0:48 – 0:51 — *Diya ban jata hai*
> Match cut: a single clay diya fills the lower third of the frame, its tall gold flame exactly where the
> sun was. Deep indigo night, layered navy bands, a crescent moon at upper right, twenty-six four-point
> stars switching on, a dark ghat silhouette, warm amber halo around the flame.
>
> **Motion:** flame flickers, stars pop on one by one, halo breathes.

### 09 · 0:51 – 0:59 — *Neend mein subah*
> The same walking composition as shot 04, now pre-dawn: sky bands from deep blue to violet to rose to
> saffron, a thin crescent moon, fading stars, the same houses and trees in muted plum. Dadi in slate
> blue carries the daura, Maa in dusty rose holds Gudiya's hand, Gudiya walks with her eyes closed,
> one flower in her free hand. The river road still curves in at the right.
>
> **Motion:** identical side-scroll and bob as shot 04 so the two shots rhyme.

### 10 · 0:59 – 1:10 — *Usha arghya*
> Sunrise. A great sun half-risen with a calm face and triangular rays; Maa stands waist-deep in the
> river lifting the soop toward it, Dadi and Gudiya behind her with folded hands. A wide golden ray
> sweeps down across the water and leaves glowing Madhubani lotus, fish and dot patterns in its path.
> In the lower left, Gudiya looks at a large round sun reflected in the water.
>
> **Motion:** the first ray grows across the water, gold patterns bloom where it passes, soop rises
> slowly, ripples spread.

### 11 · 1:10 – 1:13 — *Teen peedhiyan*
> Ivory title card with a double ink-and-vermilion border, a large gold sun with a patterned ring of
> dots and a ring of sixteen small vermilion circles. Maa in red, Dadi in green, Gudiya in pink stand in
> a row, hands folded. A dashed vermilion line and two lotus flowers at the bottom.
>
> **Type (set in the edit, not in the model):**  
> **छठ — आस्था की रोशनी**  
> **पीढ़ी-दर-पीढ़ी**

### 12 · 1:13 – 1:15 — *Loop*
> The exact opening frame of shot 01 — giant banana leaf, ivory veins, golden drop — but the great sun is
> folding itself down into that single drop. Fish and a lotus in the corners, deep green field.
>
> **Motion:** the sun scales down into the drop, one pulse of light, freeze on the frame that matches
> shot 01 for a seamless loop.

## Making it feel like one reel

- **Character sheet first.** Generate one image with Maa, Dadi and Gudiya side by side on ivory. Feed it
  as a reference to every shot that has people in it.
- **Lock the palette.** Ivory `#f8efdb` · saffron `#f2a03d` · vermilion `#cf3f2e` · leaf `#3d7a4c` ·
  gold `#e8b04b` · indigo `#18213d` · night `#0c1129`.
- **Cuts on the vocal, not on the beat grid.** The hook lands at 0:00, 0:59 and 1:10 — the three
  strongest moments get the sun.
- **Two long holds.** 0:33–0:45 and 0:59–1:10 are the emotional centre; resist cutting inside them.
- **Loop pair.** Shots 12 and 01 must share framing, scale and leaf position, or the loop will jump.
